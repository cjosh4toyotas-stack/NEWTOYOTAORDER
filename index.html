How the order works

Toyota allocates vehicles to each dealer based on market demand and available supply, and we submit your build preferences against that allocation. Your specifications go in as our request, and we match them as closely and as quickly as Toyota's build mix allows. You'll hear from us as the allocation comes through, the build locks, and the vehicle ships.

Price

The estimated MSRP is based on Toyota's pricing on the day the order is placed. Toyota may make incremental price adjustments throughout the year. The vehicle is sold at the MSRP in effect when it arrives.

Delivery

The estimated delivery date is our best estimate from current production and shipping trends. The vehicle may arrive earlier or later. We will update you as it is built, shipped and received.

Partial payment

A non-refundable $1,000 partial payment places the order. It is not a fee; it applies to the purchase price at delivery. If your needs change before arrival, it transfers to another vehicle purchased from McGrath Toyota of Iowa City. If you decide not to purchase from McGrath Toyota of Iowa City, it is forfeited.

Order accuracy

When Toyota assigns an allocation to your order, we confirm the build with you while the change window is open and before it is locked for production.
